## Visuals

  1. local Git diff view

  3. pull request review

## Notes

- Post from your personal account.
- `Introducing Reviu` is the strongest default hook for a launch-day post.
- `Today I'm launching Reviu` feels more personal and works better if you want the tweet to sound founder-led.
- Lead with what Reviu is, not with a vague launch phrase.
- Keep one clear CTA per post.
- Ask for feedback, especially from developers who review pull requests often.
- If launching on Product Hunt the same day, post the website first or Product Hunt first depending on the main goal for that day.
- Reply quickly to comments during the first few hours.

## Style References

- Recent X launch posts commonly use a clear launch hook, short founder context, a concrete feature list, media, and a direct feedback/support CTA.
- Product launch examples emphasize an "Introducing" or launch-day hook, a concise value proposition, visuals, and one CTA.
- Product threads work best as short sequences where each post acts like one slide, with media and the CTA near the end.
